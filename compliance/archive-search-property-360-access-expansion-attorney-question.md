# Archive Search — Quick Question: Expanding Access via Property 360

**Prepared for:** Peter McKenzie, Rincon Management, to forward to outside counsel
**Date:** 2026-09-23
**Purpose:** one short, specific question — not a re-review of anything already approved.

---

Your 9/23 opinion approved releasing Archive Search's AI-flagged conversations into ordinary search without individual human review, and gave Rincon standing authority to make similar changes going forward — conditioned explicitly on "the same authorized personnel remain[ing] the users." Right now, that population is exactly 2 people: both named admins, both granted the same day. (Every document up to today had assumed roughly 8 authorized users; the real number, checked directly against the database, is 2.)

**Rincon wants to add a search box to Property 360** — a per-property summary page — placed under that page's Maintenance Notes section, wired to Archive Search's existing search function. Property 360 itself has no access gate of its own: anyone with a Hub login can open it (currently 9 active logins, a number that grows automatically as staff are hired, with no admission step of its own). Rincon's intent is for the search box to work the same way — usable by anyone who can open Property 360, not gated to the 2 current Archive Search admins.

**The question:** does your opinion's standing authority extend to this — expanding Archive Search's authorized-user population from 2 named admins to an open-ended, headcount-linked group with no vetting or admission step — or is this the kind of "materially broader employee population" change your opinion named as still requiring its own review?

**One related, practical question, only if your answer to the above is that this needs something more than a bare expansion:** is there a version of this that would work — for example, a real intermediate access tier (something short of full admin, but still a defined, granted role) with baseline training as a condition of getting it — or does the underlying safeguard your opinion leans on (trained employees, an escalation path with something to escalate to) require more than Rincon has any of today?

Rincon's owner has separately signed a document accepting the risk of proceeding without waiting for this answer, so this question isn't gating whether Rincon can decide — it's gating what Rincon's own governance reviewers can sign off on before anything gets built.
