**Purpose:** one specific new question, separate from the privilege question above and from any prior approval of live-mail handling.

**The question:** is it safe for Rincon's system to generate and save that kind of note automatically, unreviewed, about old mail — or should the AI only flag the category and send it to a human, with a person deciding whether any note gets written at all?
