Situation Overview
• Rincon Management, based in Ventura County, California, is committed to delivering exceptional owner and
tenant experiences. They emphasize transparent communication, strong values, and education for landlords.
• Rincon prides itself on its straightforward pricing model, devoid of any hidden fees. This commitment to honesty
fosters trust and cultivates enduring relationships with clients who appreciate the clarity and integrity with which
their business is conducted.
• Despite their success and current growth trajectory, Rincon has some ambitious goals.
• The vision is to be Ventura County's premier resource for property management. While their immediate objective
is to grow the portfolio to 1,000 properties under management, the ultimate objective is to scale to 4,500
properties. The current growth rate of won’t get them there fast enough.
• Despite a recent growth rate of 30% annually, current strategies are falling short of expectations.
• The immediate challenge is to generate more qualified leads to meet these ambitious targets.
• Management recognizes that improved marketing will be key to achieving this growth and has committed to
investing in building the marketing team, capabilities, processes and programs needed to deliver the desired
result.

Go to Market Approach
• Build and launch an ongoing targeted outbound campaign flow where we test/learn and repeat to grow a reliable stream of new leads.
• Our campaign objectives are twofold -
1. Convince a self-managing landlord to use a PM company, and to use Rincon (concept & competitive sale)
2. Convince a landlord currently using another PM company to switch to Rincon (competitive sale)
• We have a set offers we can use as promotional hooks and reasons to act.
• Our challenge is to build awareness of what a property management company can do for you, how Rincon is different, and then stay in
front of all who engage with us to be top of mind when they need us.
• Self-managing landlords (both accidentals and investors) may be easier to convince that Rincon can immediately solve their landlord pain.
• Those already using a property management company they are unhappy with can be an attractive switch offer opportunity
• Our targeted mediums for reaching investors and accidental landlords are search, social channels, email and direct mail. Direct mail is a
consideration as we suspect many in the target audience are older and less digitally savvy and that DM stands out in increasingly empty
mailboxes.
• We also must launch ongoing nurture efforts for current contacts and those added MQLs that don’t make it through the sales funnel.
• New demand generation efforts will grow the contact list significantly. Our lead engine will become a combination of the above targeted
outbound, and SEO driven traffic, with paid ads layered on as we develop reliable messaging, offers and lead acquisition cost and
conversion metrics.
• Lead generation successes will provide the fuel ($) for ramping the investment in marketing programs and resources.

Account Based Marketing (ABM)
• ABM, or Account Based Marketing, is the practice of using the CRM
platform (Hubspot) to automate the process of nurturing target accounts
by sending relevant and engaging content with the aim of increasing their
engagement with the brand and becoming a customer.
• In doing so we are looking to move contacts through the marketing funnel.
• While ABM programs generally focus on the top of the funnel, by educating
them throughout the process we increase the propensity of them moving
through the bottom of the funnel and become customers.
• Delivering the ABM program requires a combination of strategic thinking to
identify target accounts and create relevant, persuasive content to engage
contacts and move them through the sales funnel.
• Technology is then used to manage the program, providing insights and
analytics needed to optimize it on a continuous basis.
