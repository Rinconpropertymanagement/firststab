Inspection Coordinator
How to upload declaration pages

A declaration page is the first page of an insurance certificate. It shows the property address, the insurer, the policy number, and the expiration date. You upload these so the system can read and store them automatically.

  1. Click the blue Batch Upload button in the top-right corner of the page.
  2. A window will appear. Click Click to select files, or drag your PDF or image files directly into the box. You can upload up to 50 files at once.
  3. After selecting files, they will appear in a list below the box. You can remove any file by clicking the small X next to its name.
  4. When your list is ready, click Upload & Extract. The system will read each file and try to fill in the details automatically. This usually takes 10–30 seconds per file.
  5. Once done, a review table appears. Check that each row shows the correct property. If the system guessed wrong, click the property name to search for the right one.
  6. Check the Add'l Insured checkbox if the insurance certificate shows Rincon Management as an Additional Insured.
  7. When everything looks correct, click Save Selected. The records will go into the system with a status of “Pending Review” for your property manager to check.
How to read the dashboard

The main dashboard shows all properties and their insurance status.

  • Green (Compliant) — the policy is current and meets all requirements.
  • Orange (Expiring Soon) — the policy expires within 30 days. A renewal is needed.
  • Red (Expired) — the policy has already expired.
  • Gray (Pending Review) — the document was uploaded but has not been reviewed by a property manager yet.
  • Red with border (Low Liability) — the coverage amount is below $500,000.
  • Orange with border (No Add'l Insured) — Rincon Management is not listed as an additional insured on the policy.

Click on any row to see more details about that property's policy.

What to do when the system cannot find a property match

Sometimes the address on the insurance certificate is written differently from how it appears in our system. When this happens, the Property column will show a search box instead of a property name.

  1. Type the street number or a few words from the property address into the search box.
  2. A dropdown list will appear with matching properties. Click the correct one.
  3. If you are not sure which property it is, check the “Address on Policy” field shown in the extracted details column.
  4. If you cannot find a match, leave that row unchecked and let your property manager know.
Property Manager
How to review and approve a policy

After an inspection coordinator uploads a declaration page, it appears in the Review Queue tab with a status of “Pending Review.” Your job is to confirm that the policy is valid before it is marked as compliant.

  1. Click the Review Queue tab at the top of the page.
  2. You will see a list of policies waiting for review. Each row shows the property, insurer, expiration date, and what the system thinks the status is.
  3. If the policy looks correct and meets your requirements, click Approve. You can add a note if needed.
  4. If the policy is compliant, it will be marked as such. If the system flagged it as non-compliant (expired, low liability, or missing additional insured), approving it will send the record to the Director of Operations for a second review.
  5. If the document is wrong or was uploaded by mistake, click Reject and enter a brief reason.
How to verify the Additional Insured and Coverage checkboxes

On the main dashboard, click any property row to expand it. You will see two checkboxes in the detail panel.

  • Additional Insured — Verified: Check this once you have confirmed that Rincon Management (or the correct entity) is listed as an additional insured on the policy document.
  • Premises Liability — Verified: Check this once you have confirmed that the liability coverage amount meets the minimum requirement ($500,000).

These checkboxes save automatically the moment you click them. No need to press a save button.

Understanding escalated policies

When a policy is flagged as non-compliant (expired, low liability, or no additional insured) and you approve it anyway, it becomes Escalated. This sends an email to the Director of Operations and places it in their review queue. You do not need to do anything further — the Director will take it from there.

Director of Operations
How to confirm a non-compliant policy

When a property manager approves a policy that the system flagged as non-compliant, it is escalated to you. You must review it and make a final decision.

  1. Click the Review Queue tab. Escalated records appear with a purple “Escalated” badge.
  2. Review the property, insurer, expiration date, and coverage amount.
  3. If you agree the policy is non-compliant and want to record it as such, click Confirm Non-Compliant. The record will be updated to its final status (Expired, Low Liability, or No Additional Insured).
  4. If the document turns out to be a bad upload or duplicate, click Reject to remove it from the active records.

Note: Confirming non-compliant does not automatically contact the tenant. That is a separate step handled by the property manager or compliance team.

Understanding the escalation email

When a policy is escalated, you will receive an email at the DO email address on file. The email includes the property address, insurer, policy number, expiration date, coverage amount, and the AI-suggested status. You can then log in to the dashboard to take action.

How to add a new user

Only people with a @rinconmanagement.com email can be added, and they need to have logged into the Rincon Hub at least once first (same shared email + password as every other hub tool — there is no separate sign-in for Insurance Compliance).

  1. Click the Users tab at the top of the page.
  2. Type the person's full email address (must end in @rinconmanagement.com) into the email field.
  3. Select their role from the dropdown:
    • Inspection Coordinator — can upload documents and view the dashboard. Cannot approve or reject.
    • Property Manager — can approve and reject policies in the review queue.
    • Director of Operations — can confirm non-compliant policies after PM escalation.
    • Admin — full access, including user management.
  4. Click Add User. The person can now open Insurance Compliance from the Rincon Hub home page — nothing else to set up.
How to change someone's role
  1. Click the Users tab.
  2. Find the person in the list. Their current role appears in a dropdown in the Role column.
  3. Click the dropdown and select the new role. The change saves immediately.
How to remove a user
  1. Click the Users tab.
  2. Find the person and click the red Remove button on their row.
  3. Confirm the removal. They will no longer be able to open Insurance Compliance (they can still sign in to the Rincon Hub itself and use any other section they have access to).

Note: You cannot remove your own account. If you need to remove yourself, ask another admin to do it.
