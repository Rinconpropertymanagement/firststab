Pod Lead
How the queue works

Every open disposition case is listed sorted by how many days are left on the 21-day legal deadline — the most urgent case is always at the top. A case is created automatically the night a tenant’s move-out date is recorded, so you don’t need to start anything yourself.

Click any row to open the full packet: leaseholders, the deposit and prepaid rent figures, matched move-in/move-out photos, inspection form uploads, and the compliance checklist.

What the warning flags mean

Anything the tool couldn’t find — move-in photos, a deposit amount, an inspection form — is called out at the top of the case in an amber warning box, not hidden or shown as a passing checkmark. Review each flag before finishing the case.

Confirming tenancy status

Every case needs you to confirm whether the whole tenancy is ending or just one co-tenant is moving out while the lease continues for the others — this changes whether the 21-day clock and inspection rights apply. The tool may suggest an answer from the sync data, but you always confirm it yourself.

Uploading inspection forms

Download the move-in and move-out inspection forms from AppFolio (where you can already see them today), then attach them here as a PDF or image — one for move-in, one for move-out.

Answering the checklist

Three questions, using Mason’s finalized wording. Answers save the moment you click Yes or No — no separate save button.

Entering the Prepaid Rent balance

Look up “2300 — Prepaid Rent” for this tenant in AppFolio and type the balance into the Prepaid Rent card (enter 0 if none exists), then click Save. This tool can’t pull that figure automatically, so it’s a required manual step — you can’t click Mark Reviewed until it’s entered. Already entered it but need to fix a typo? Click Correct this figure to change it.

Marking a case reviewed, or escalating it

Once you’ve gone through everything, click Mark Reviewed. If something needs a second set of eyes — a complicated dispute, evidence you’re unsure how to weigh — click Escalate and explain why. That notifies the Director of Operations.

Director of Operations
Resolving an escalated case

Escalated cases show a purple “Escalated” badge. Open the case, review what the pod lead flagged, and click Resolve Escalation with your notes. This returns the case to Pending Review for the pod lead to finish — it does not itself decide any deduction.

Admin
Managing user access

Only @rinconmanagement.com accounts that have already logged into the Rincon Hub once can be added. Use the Users tab to add, change, or remove someone’s role.

  • Pod Lead — reviews cases, answers the checklist, uploads inspection forms, escalates.
  • Director of Operations — resolves escalated cases.
  • Admin — full access, including user management.
