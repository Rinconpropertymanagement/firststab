## 5. Aircall → HubSpot double call logging (investigated 2026-11-04, read-only)

Method: pulled every HubSpot call record for sample weeks via the connector and grouped
records with the same minute, same from-number and same to-number. Two records in a group
with identical duration = one call logged twice.

| Sample week | Distinct calls | Logged twice |
|---|---|---|
| Mar 9–13 | 12 (all hand-logged, pre-Aircall) | 0 |
| May 11–15 | ~60 | 0 |
| Jul 13–15 | 106 | 92 |
| Aug 17–21 | 126 | 72 (Aug 17–18 clean, Aug 19 on doubled) |
| Sep 21–25 | 89 | 73 |
| Oct 5–9 | 100 | 77 |
| Oct 12–16 | 127 | 73 |
| Oct 19–23 | ~200 | ~180 |
| Oct 26–29 (to 13:35 PT) | ~110 | 89 |
| Oct 29 13:43 PT – Nov 4 | ~100 | 0 |

Facts:
- Both copies of a doubled call were created by "Aircall : AI-powered calling and SMS"
  (hs_object_source_label INTEGRATION, hs_call_source INTEGRATIONS_PLATFORM), within
  ~50 ms of each other, same owner, same duration, same recording. Not a workflow, not a
  user, not the hub's scripts.
- Only calls on the Office Line (+1 805 288 1119, Dio's line) were doubled. Calls to the
  phone tree (+1 805 288 1190) and the AI agents were logged once throughout.
- Last doubled call: 2026-10-29 20:33 UTC (13:33 PT). Every call since 20:43 UTC that day
  is logged once.
- HubSpot → Connected Apps shows "Aircall : AI-powered calling and SMS" with Installed
  Date **Oct 29, 2026, installed by Peter McKenzie** — i.e. the Aircall app was
  (re)installed that afternoon, which is exactly when the doubling stopped.
- Aircall's status page shows "Increased Platform Errors" Oct 29 21:12–23:44 UTC (after the
  doubling had already stopped). That incident explains a different, small artefact: 8 calls
  from Oct 29 were logged a second time on Oct 30 ~14:00–18:30 UTC (Aircall re-sent them on
  recovery). It does not explain the months of doubling.

Conclusion (confirmed by Peter 2026-11-04): the Aircall→HubSpot integration was connected
twice, so each call was pushed twice. Peter disconnected one of the two connections on
Oct 29, which is why the doubling stopped that afternoon. It ran from roughly June/July
2026 to Oct 29. Start date not pinned to the day (May is clean, July is doubled; nothing in
between was sampled, and Peter does not need it pinned further).

Rough size: ~75–90 extra call records per working week × ~17 weeks ≈ **1,300–1,500 extra
call records** in HubSpot, all on the Office Line. Impact: any report or scorecard that
counts HubSpot call records for Dio (calls made/received) is roughly doubled for Jul–Oct.
Call recordings and contacts are unaffected.

Going forward: fixed by Peter on Oct 29 (one connection removed). Nothing more to change.
