## A note on the two free-text fields above

Writing "in your own words" isn't just about keeping this fair to the AI (which is also
required to paraphrase, never quote verbatim) — it's also so this file itself doesn't
become a place where an applicant's exact words end up sitting in a plain text file outside
LeadSimple. If a field mentions anything about the applicant's income source, disability,
family situation, immigration status, or anything else that feels sensitive or personal,
still summarize it plainly and factually (e.g. "mentioned rent will be paid via a housing
voucher") — don't leave it out, and don't editorialize about it either. Just state what the
field says, briefly, the same way you'd describe it to a coworker over the shoulder.
